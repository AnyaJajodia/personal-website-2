The imaging workstation

Each tray of dragonfly records goes under an overhead camera. BugBase runs the sequence: the tray is positioned, the specimen and its label are photographed, and the images are checked before they’re stored.

I operate the setup; the rig, trays and software were built by others in the lab.

Prototype only: the toolbar at the top is for comparing options and isn’t part of the page. Labels, markers and plate references are HTML, so they stay sharp and readable at every size; the artwork and photographs carry no baked-in text.
