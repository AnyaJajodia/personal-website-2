# American Museum of Natural History

## Behind the collections. Into new questions.

Collections digitization and emerging behavioral research in Invertebrate Zoology at the American Museum of Natural History.

**Role:** Volunteer  
**Period:** 2026–present  
**Lab:** Jessica Ware’s lab · Invertebrate Zoology  
**Location:** New York

## Two threads at the museum

Beyond its public galleries, AMNH is a place of scientific collections and ongoing research. I volunteer in Jessica Ware’s lab, where my work is beginning to span both: helping digitize dragonfly specimens and joining a behavioral research project.

### DIODE · Collections digitization

I prepare specimens and their collection labels for imaging, operate the existing capture setup, review the resulting images, and rehouse the specimens afterward. It is a hands-on process connecting physical collections with digital records.

### Behavioral research

I’m joining a project investigating feeding behavior. More on the research and my contribution once the work begins.

**Explore behavioral research ↗**

## Following the questions further

I came to the museum wanting to spend more time with questions about animals and their behavior. Working with the collections has given me another way into those questions—and a closer look at the care and tools behind the records researchers use.
