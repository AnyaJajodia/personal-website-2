# Lynxster

## Building systems of trust.

Engineering, product development, and technical leadership for independently
verifiable digital records.

**Role:** Chief Technology Officer & Co-founder  
**Period:** April 2026–present  
**Status:** Patent pending

## The problem

Organizations rely on digital records every day. When a record moves between
people or organizations, confirming that it is genuine and unaltered can
become a complicated process.

Lynxster is building infrastructure intended to make important digital
records independently verifiable.

## From developer to co-founder

I joined Lynxster in April 2026 as a Junior Developer, when the company was
still at an early stage. Starting with a blank slate, I worked across the
technical foundations and gradually took on responsibilities beyond
development.

Today, as Chief Technology Officer and Co-founder, I work across engineering,
product, and the systems that support the company’s development.

## What I work on

### Engineering and infrastructure

I’ve built foundational software and set up development infrastructure across
the company’s technology stack, helping establish the environment for ongoing
product work.

### Cryptography and product

My work has included cryptographic implementation and product development:
moving between foundational technical questions and the application being
built around them.

### Agent-assisted development

I established an AI-assisted development environment to support engineering
work, documentation, iteration, and even interally for the other teams at Lynxster.

### Product and communication

Alongside development, I’ve worked on technical documentation, demonstrations,
outreach, and communicating the product’s purpose.

I also prepared the first complete draft of the patent application, which
became the starting point for subsequent work and revisions by the wider team.

## What I’m learning

Working at an early-stage company has meant moving between building,
explaining, and making decisions about what comes next.

I’m learning how to bring technical work and product thinking together while
helping shape a company from its early stages.

## More to come

Lynxster is ongoing and patent pending. More details will be shared as the
work develops.
